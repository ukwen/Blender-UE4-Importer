Recovery plans and status are scoped to the scanned project descriptor, selected level and resolved external recovery folder. The sidebar offers Read Recovery Plan and Copy, shows verified/ambiguous/not found/unsupported/blocked counts and hides Restore when nothing is verified. Review stores a digest; a changed plan or superseded confirmation is refused. Older plans have no scope metadata and require locating again. Restore still recomputes the full plan and qualifies every original candidate before no-clobber publication. Folder enumeration errors fail closed; skipped nonregular/symlink and unidentified files are counted, as are packages not matching recoverable dependencies. This is not a sandbox or protection against hostile concurrent edits; unknown package identity cannot be inferred.
